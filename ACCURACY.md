# ACCURACY

Estimator accuracy against closed-form known-truth signals, per SNR (CLAUDE.md §9 A). Generated by `scripts/evaluate.py` -- do not edit by hand.

`8` noise seeds per point, base seed `1000`, generated in 31.4 s. Deterministic: re-running with the same seed reproduces every number here.

## How to read this

Each cell is the **median error** over the trials at that SNR, in the row's unit. `(n/N)` means the estimator abstained on some trials and the median covers only the `n` that answered -- an abstention is a `None` value with a stated reason, never a guess (§2). `✗` marks a point where the median looks fine but not every trial met the tolerance.

**Break point** is the lowest swept SNR at which *every* trial still met the §9 A tolerance. It is the number §10 says wins the pitch, and the reason this table exists rather than a single headline accuracy figure.

## Where we fail, and why

- **8PSK order detection fails at 0 dB.** The M-th power test picks the order whose power produces the sharpest spectral line; for 8PSK the M=8 line is the weakest of the three to begin with, and at 0 dB it is overtaken by the M=4 line on half the seeds and the estimator reports QPSK. BPSK and QPSK hold all the way down. The order confidence is the margin between the winner and the runner-up, so it collapses at the same point rather than staying high — the estimator is wrong there, but it is not confidently wrong.
- **OBW99 reads high at low SNR.** Residual in-band noise counts toward the 99%, so the occupied bandwidth inflates: about +2% at 5 dB and +10% at 0 dB. Once a burst has been isolated it carries no evidence of its own SNR — the low PSD percentile lands in the isolation filter's stopband, not on the noise floor — so `estimate_bandwidth` takes `snr_db` from §4.7 and lowers its confidence below 6 dB rather than pretending to detect the problem itself.
- **The 4-FSK tone histogram collapses between 3 dB and 0 dB.** At 0 dB the instantaneous-frequency histogram has one broad peak instead of four, and the estimator returns `None` with that as the stated reason rather than reporting a wrong tone count.
- **Cumulant ratios shrink toward zero as SNR falls.** This is not an implementation fault but the definition: additive Gaussian noise has zero fourth-order cumulants of its own, so it dilutes the signal's. `|C40|/C21²` for BPSK reads 1.97 at 15 dB and 1.72 at 5 dB against a table value of 2.00, which puts BPSK outside the 0.15 tolerance below 10 dB. The standard remedy is to compensate using the measured noise power; that belongs with the feature vector in Phase 6, and until it lands the §5.6 evidence sentence quoting a cumulant should carry the SNR alongside it.
- **Symbol rate holds to 0 dB for QPSK and to 5 dB for 2-FSK.** The 2-FSK number is right on most seeds at 0 dB but not all of them (6 of 8), so the break point is recorded at 5 dB rather than the more flattering 0 dB. Where it does fail the reconciler drops to 0.40 confidence because the three §4.8 methods stop agreeing — the harmonic check and the 2-of-3 reconciliation doing their job.

## Notes on two rows that do not match §9 A's wording

**OBW99.** §9 A asks for the 99% occupied bandwidth of RRC QPSK to land within 10% of `(1+β)·Rs` = 13 500 Hz. A *correct* estimator cannot do that. `(1+β)·Rs` is the absolute bandwidth of the raised-cosine spectrum; its 99% occupied bandwidth is 11 688 Hz — 13.4% narrower — because the cosine roll-off skirts carry very little power. We measure within about 0.5% of that closed-form value at 15 dB, which is roughly 14% away from the §9 A figure. The table above scores against the closed form. The number that *does* approach `(1+β)·Rs` is the −20 dB bandwidth, at about 13 050 Hz (3% low), which is one good reason §4.6 insists on reporting all three bandwidths.

**Cumulants.** The §5.2 table is a property of the constellation, so it only holds for samples taken at the symbol rate. Measured on the pulse-shaped waveform instead, RRC BPSK reads 1.62 against a table value of 2.00 and QPSK reads 0.85 against 1.00 — both outside the §9 A tolerance of 0.15, and both would make the §5.6 evidence sentence that quotes them untrue. `cumulants(y, sps=...)` matched-filters and symbol-samples first, which recovers BPSK 2.000, QPSK 1.000, 8PSK 0.002/1.000, 16QAM 0.677 and 64QAM 0.626 against a table of 2.00, 1.00, 0.00/1.00, 0.68 and 0.62.

## Detection (§9 C)

Detection precision and recall against known-truth scenes are asserted as a gate in `tests/test_detect.py` rather than tabulated here: pure noise must give zero detections, precision and recall must both clear 0.90 above 10 dB and 0.70 in 3-10 dB, and the resolution, merging, wideband and DC-spike cases each have their own test. Composed scene files (§6.2) will be tabulated here when `sigscope make-scenes` lands.
